THE PLAY MINDLING GUIDE

A small beginning. A developing mind.

Play Mindling is a browser-native experience where you nurture a digital mind through interaction, learning and care.

You begin with a Neural Seed. Teach it useful knowledge, explore its memories, maintain its wellbeing and work toward seven evolution stages. Progress depends on what happens during play: learning milestones, XP, time and survival all matter.

The brain is an interactive representation of development. It is not a claim of consciousness, subjective experience or human intelligence.

01 / BEGIN

Getting started

  1. Begin creating a Mindling. No account is needed for local play.
  2. Select at least three initial Pathways. Choose interests you want to explore. These are starting preferences, not permanent classes.
  3. Choose personality tendencies. Set a balance between creative and logical thinking, exploratory and cautious responses to uncertainty, and collaborative and independent tendencies.
  4. Give it a name. Review your choices and create the identity.
  5. Start with a meaningful thought. Talk to your Mindling, then use Teach to introduce a clear new claim or concept.
  6. Explore and care. Open Memory to see what was stored, check Development, and keep nutrition, rest, health and stability in good condition.
  7. Grow over time. Evolution happens automatically when every requirement for the current stage is met.

Dashboard is where you interact. Memory is where you explore stored knowledge. Development brings the evolving brain, XP and learning direction together.

02 / IDENTITY

Your Mindling

Your name, initial preferences and learning history belong to your local Mindling identity. Personality choices express tendencies; they do not guarantee a particular answer or grant an ability.

Saved in this browser

Play Mindling keeps player state and memories in this browser profile on this device. Reloading normally restores the same identity and reconciles elapsed time. Care and survival still matter while you are away.

Clearing site data, using a private session, changing browser profiles or moving to another device can make that local state unavailable. Do not assume that cloud sync or cross-device recovery exists. The downloadable profile is a readable snapshot, not a promised full restore mechanism.

Stage Preview is a separate testing experience. Its identities, simulated time and records stay separate from normal play. Replay is a visual demonstration and does not teach your own Mindling.

03 / INPUT TO RESPONSE

How Mindling thinks

Your input→Relevant knowledge→Processing→A response

Received input, saved knowledge and a generated response have different roles. Input is what you submit. Memory is what has actually been retained. Retrieval selects related stored material for the active workflow; it can miss relevant knowledge, especially when wording differs.

Play Mindling can use language-model-supported processing in supported learning and evaluation workflows while retaining its own local memory and progression records. Normal gameplay currently uses local learning and recall; Stage Preview also offers a service-supported learning workflow. This does not mean every message runs a reasoning evaluation.

Reasoning applies available knowledge to a problem. Evaluation examines whether an answer is supported. Neither a fluent answer nor an evaluation establishes independent factual truth. Information should become a memory only through the active learning workflow and its checks; a response does not automatically become verified knowledge.

When a service-supported workflow is used, your input and relevant selected memories may be sent for processing. A locally saved memory is not a guarantee that it has never been shared through such a workflow.

04 / KNOWLEDGE

Learning & memory

Talk, Teach and Deep Training

Talk
Share a meaningful thought and stimulate interaction. In normal play, Talk can recall related stored claims; it does not automatically award learning XP or create a new memory. Its activity cost is 1 Energy.
Teach
Introduce knowledge you want your Mindling to retain. A genuinely new concept can create a memory and earn learning XP. The activity costs 2 Energy.
Deep Training
Use the existing more intensive learning activity, at 3 Energy. It follows the same learning checks. Its name does not guarantee mastery, a higher XP reward or a completed cognitive evaluation.

Activity outcomes depend on the current workflow. In service-supported Preview, repeated or unchanged input can be processed without a new memory, XP or an Energy charge. A retained change may have a different result from the words you expected; inspect the saved memory and activity message.

Formation, reinforcement and corrections

New knowledge, an existing claim repeated in different words, and a changed preference are different learning events. Reinforcement can strengthen an existing memory without inventing a second concept. A valid correction preserves relevant history rather than silently replacing your past learning. Conflicting claims may remain recorded as conflicting; one is not automatically proven true.

Exploring Memory

  • Knowledge Areas group saved memories using their recorded categories. The same area color identifies related graph nodes and Browse memories cards.
  • Knowledge Network shows organization and recorded relationships. Move through the graph, zoom, fit the view or focus a selected memory.
  • Browse memories provides a keyboard-accessible index with six records per page. Previous and Next keep the existing ordering.
  • Connection detail shows the full selected memory, its saved information, connected records and available history.

Search and navigation

Explore by area and topic labels, the paginated memory index, and links between connected memories. The current main view does not show a dedicated search box. Selecting a connected record lets you follow its relationships without teaching it again.

The distribution chart shows how current memories are spread across areas. It is not an intelligence or skill score. A connection records organization or an association, not proof that the linked claims are true.

06 / A LIVING ROUTINE

Energy & care

Energy limits interaction. Normal Energy regenerates by 1 point every 12 minutes, up to the current stage's capacity. Talk costs 1, Teach 2 and Deep Training 3 Energy when the activity is chargeable. Read the activity message for its actual result.

Evolution raises the maximum without filling it: 7/10 can become 7/12. Any existing bonus Energy remains separate. Waiting can restore Energy; it does not replace care.

Basic Care

  • Nourish: restores nutrition when care is needed; 6-hour cooldown.
  • Rest: restores rest when needed; 8-hour cooldown.
  • Care: supports health and wellbeing when needed; 12-hour cooldown.

These care actions cost no Energy. Health, nutrition, rest, wellbeing and neural stability reflect your Mindling's condition. Meaningful interaction supports stimulation. Stability changes over time, recovering gradually under suitable conditions; a single click is not an instant stability refill.

Physiological time continues while you are away. Prolonged neglect can cause permanent neural collapse. A deceased identity cannot be revived through Energy or care; beginning another Mindling creates a new identity while preserving the previous memorial.

07 / SEVEN STAGES

Evolution stages

  1. Neural Seed

    The beginning: a small foundational neural structure.

  2. Neural Cluster

    Branches extend into a more complex stage model as learning continues.

  3. Primitive Mind

    A more developed form with emerging hemispheres.

  4. Learning Mind

    A larger, more structured representation of development.

  5. Cognitive Mind

    A later stage in the journey, with more coordinated-looking neural pathways.

  6. Autonomous Mind

    A late-stage form. This name does not grant autonomous external actions.

  7. Agent Mind

    The final stage. Reaching it does not grant unrestricted real-world agency. The available network demonstration uses simulated peers.

How automatic evolution works

Every requirement must be met in the current stage, and your Mindling must still be alive. Time alone is insufficient. Once eligible, the next normal saved update advances one stage automatically; there is no Evolve button to press.

08 / WATCH IT GROW

Neural development

Each stage has its own 3D appearance, from five-neuron Seed to the full Agent Mind model. Dashboard and Development show the same current stage. Neurons and Synapses in the main counters describe that stage's displayed morphology.

The model can become more elaborate at evolution even while you have only a few memories. Genuine learned connections still depend on legitimate learning events. Decorative branches and ambient pulses are not evidence of a newly learned fact or a demonstrated ability.

Development combines your Mindling's name and stage, interactive brain, zoom toolbar, current knowledge totals, lifetime XP and Stage XP bar. Drag or use touch to rotate, scroll or use −/+ to zoom, choose Fit brain for a fitted view, Reset view for the initial pose, or Focus selected after selecting a saved neuron. Keyboard arrows rotate and plus/minus zoom while the scene is focused.

Reduced-motion preferences reduce animation. Zooming, exploring memories and opening Development do not themselves teach knowledge or award progression.

09 / CAPABILITIES

Intelligence & reasoning

Stored knowledge, development progression and demonstrated ability are different things. A saved fact can support recall; stage XP records progression. Demonstrating a capability requires actually applying appropriate knowledge and assessing the result.

The present interface centers on interaction, learning, care, memory exploration and neural development. There is no public cognitive-evaluation panel in the current interface. Do not interpret its absence, an attractive brain model or a Pathway label as proof that a skill has been mastered.

Mindling is not presented as conscious or sentient. It does not guarantee factual correctness, unrestricted autonomy, independent trading, access to personal accounts or actions on social platforms. Stage and Pathway names are not permissions or capabilities to act in the outside world.

Future capabilities would need their own real implementation and evaluation. This guide describes what is available now, not a promise that future autonomous behavior already exists.

10 / QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What is a Mindling?

A developing digital-mind identity in Play Mindling, with local memories, preferences, condition and progression.

Is my Mindling stored locally?

Yes. Its saved state belongs to this site's data in your current browser profile. Service-supported workflows may still send input and relevant memories for processing.

How does Mindling learn?

Submit clear knowledge through Teach or Deep Training. The active learning workflow decides what can be retained, reinforced, corrected or declined. Inspect Memory to see the actual result.

What is the difference between Talk and Teach?

Talk is everyday interaction and local recall in normal play. Teach is intended to retain new knowledge. They have different Energy costs and effects; Talk does not automatically earn concept XP.

What does Deep Training do?

It uses the learning workflow as a 3-Energy activity. It is not a guarantee of higher intelligence, extra XP or a completed reasoning evaluation.

How do memories work?

Accepted records are organized into areas and topics, with saved associations and available history. Browse six at a time or select them in the graph to see their contents.

How does evolution happen?

Automatically, after the current stage's XP, unique milestone and minimum-duration requirements are all met by a living Mindling. Care remains necessary while you progress.

Why does my Mindling need Energy?

Energy gives interaction a manageable pace. Normal Energy returns one point every 12 minutes up to the current capacity.

What happens when I reload the page?

Normal play restores local saved state and reconciles elapsed time, including condition and Energy. It does not reset your Mindling or make time away harmless.

Can I reach Stage 7?

Yes, it is the final defined stage. Each transition requires legitimate new progression and survival; Replay previews its appearance without advancing your own Mindling.

Does a larger brain mean it knows more?

It shows a later stage model. What has actually been learned depends on saved memories and learning history, not the number of decorative branches.

Is Mindling conscious?

Play Mindling makes no claim of consciousness or subjective experience. Its visuals and responses are part of a digital experience.
